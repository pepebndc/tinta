Tinta · pet exploration · 25 September 2026
pluma/ˈplu.ma/

In Spanish, pluma is a feather and also a pen. Tinta is ink. A pen and its ink are a natural pair, so the pet is the thing that writes with Tinta. This page shows nine directions in two families, the quill and the stroke, with three tones of voice, and the pet in the app. All pets react to the same states. Select a state at the top, or click a pet.

Role
A quiet companion that shows what Tinta does: listen, write, summarize, rest.
Not
A chat bot, an assistant, or an AI persona. Tinta has no chat, and Pluma does not change that.
Lives in
The sidebar, the recording pill, empty states, notifications, the menu bar, and the website.
1 · Visual directions

Quill and stroke

The quill is the object. The stroke is the mark that it makes. The hybrids put both in one character. The scores are a first opinion. Vote on each card and add notes. The page keeps your votes in this browser, and the Review section exports them.

2 · Tone of voice

How much does Pluma talk?

Tinta is a privacy tool. It must feel calm and trustworthy. The tone sets how often Pluma speaks and how much personality it shows. Select a tone. The cards and the app mock use it.

Never during the callPluma does not move much or speak while you record. The meeting has your attention, not the pet.
Never pretends to thinkPluma says what the app does ("summarizing"), and never "I think" or "I understand". The on-device model does the work.
Keeps secretsPluma is local, as Tinta is. Privacy moments (encryption, audio deletion) are where its character is most useful.
Reminds about consentTinta does not tell other people that you record. Pluma is a soft way to remind you to tell them.
Always optionalA setting turns Pluma off. Then the app shows the same states with the normal labels.

4 · The name

Pluma, and its neighbors

"Pluma" is short, easy to say in English and Spanish, and it pairs with "tinta". The type samples use the same script as the Tinta wordmark, and three other voices.

5 · Recommendation and review

A first opinion

Direction: H1, Pluma y trazo

The quill gives Pluma a face and warmth. The stroke shows what Tinta does, and it matches the script wordmark. Use the quill alone in small places: the sidebar, the pill, and the menu bar. Show the line on large surfaces: the onboarding, the empty library, and the website.

Also strong: Q3 and T3

Plumín is the clearest shape at 16 points. It can be the small form of H1. Pincelada is the closest match with the wordmark. Its ink ball can become the head of the H1 line on the website.

Tone: Quiet, with Warm moments

Pluma shows the state with its posture, and the app keeps its normal labels. Warm lines appear only at the first launch, the empty library, the consent reminder, and the audio deletion.
