Tinta · logo exploration

One shape for the logo and the pet

Tinta is ink. The nib is the tool that puts the ink on paper. If the app icon is the nib, the logo and Pluma are the same object. The logo is Pluma at rest, and in the app Pluma opens its eyes. Point at an icon on this page to wake it.

4 · Recommendation and review

A first opinion

Yes, with option 2

The faceless nib is calm, like an app icon must be. It keeps the Tinta colors and the lavender feed. Pluma is the same nib with eyes, so the pet feels like the logo that comes alive.

Runner-up: option 6

The closed eyes add character without a full face. The risk: an icon with a face looks like a game or a children's app next to Mail and Calendar.

Check before the change

Nib icons are common for writing apps. Compare the shape in the App Store and in the Dock of real users. The drop of the current icon can stay as the ink drop in option 4.
