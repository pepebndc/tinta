Is a nib icon safe and different?

We compared the logo options with 80 icons. Of these, 72 were checked by eye and the rest from text descriptions. The survey covers meeting and dictation apps, writing and design apps, and pen brands. We also checked the Apple guidelines for macOS 26 and 27, and the current icon trends. This is not legal advice. Before you file a trademark, ask a lawyer.

1 · Verdict

A plain nib is not different enough

High overlap

The upright nib

Ulysses already has an upright nib with a slit and a round hole, in a light color on a dark squircle. VoiceInk, a dictation app, has the same nib motif. Meetily, a meeting app, has a white writing tool on a purple squircle.

Low legal risk

Trademarks and copyright

We found no registered mark on a generic nib or a round hole. Simple shapes get little copyright protection. The risks are specific brand features: the heart hole (Platinum), the keyhole nib (Pelikan), and a face on a nib (Pilot Kakuno).

Crowded colors

The indigo squircle

A white glyph on an indigo or violet squircle is common in this category: Jamie, Read.ai, Limitless, and Meetily. A flat color helps, because most of these apps use a gradient. The color alone does not make Tinta different.

5 · Trademarks and copyright

What is protected

Brand or ruleWhat it coversFor Tinta
MontblancThe white "snowcap" star, registered since 1913. The "4810" engraving on the nib. SourceUse no star and no numbers on the nib.
Platinum, NakayaA heart-shaped breather hole as a brand feature. SourceRemove the heart from the logo. In the pet, the heart is only an expression, so the risk is low.
PelikanThe pelican mark. The historic "keyhole" nib. SourceOption 5 (keyhole) comes near to a known pen feature. Use no bird.
Pilot KakunoA nib with an engraved smiling face. SourceKeep the pet's face on the shoulders. Do not engrave a smile on the tines.
Parker, SheafferThe arrow clip. The white dot, registered since 1924. SourceUse no arrow, and no white dot on the feed band.
EU shape marksA shape must "depart significantly from the norm" of the sector. EUIPONobody owns a generic nib. For the same reason, Tinta cannot own one.

    Apple guidelines (macOS 26 and 27)

    Build the icon in Icon Composer, with a background layer and foreground layers. The system adds the squircle, the highlights, and the glass. Supply the default, dark, clear, and tinted appearances with the same core shapes. Use clear edges, no very thin lines, and no own shadows or glows. HIG: App icons

    The slit of the nib is a thin line. Check it at 16 and 32 pixels, and in the clear and tinted appearances.
