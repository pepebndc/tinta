plumín/pluˈmin/

A plumín is the nib of a fountain pen: the metal part that touches the paper. Every nib has a slit that carries the ink, and a small hole at the top of the slit, the breather hole. On this pet, the breather hole is the mouth. It is a real hole in the metal, so it can change shape without teeth, lips, or a tongue.

1 · Anatomy

Every part is a real part of a nib

The face uses the parts that a nib already has. Nothing is added only to make it cute.

Feed
The lavender band at the top. On a pen, it holds the ink behind the nib. It carries the accent color.
Shoulders
The widest part. The eyes and the cheeks live here.
Eyes
The only part that is not on a real nib. Two paper dots, with pupils that follow the pointer.
Breather hole
The mouth. On a pen, it lets air in so the ink flows, and it stops cracks in the metal. On Pluma, it is the main tool of expression.
Slit
Carries the ink from the hole to the tip. While Tinta writes, ink flows down the slit.
Tip
Touches the paper. It is the ground line, so Pluma stands on the point where it writes.
2 · Mouth shapes

Four real breather holes, four meanings

Fountain pen nibs use round, heart, keyhole, and teardrop breather holes. Each shape can carry one meaning in Tinta. The round hole is the usual face. The other three are rare, so they stay special.

4 · The mouth as a signal

The mouth can carry data, not only mood

A hole that opens and closes is also a small meter. Two uses make the mouth useful, not only decorative.

A level meter while you record

The mouth opens with the voice level of the call. It uses the same data as the level meters in Tinta, so the face tells the truth. When the Meet extension reports that your microphone is muted, the mouth becomes a flat line and a small muted icon appears. A closed mouth for a long time says that nobody speaks.

Talking, for the Warm moments

When Pluma says a line, the mouth follows the letters: open on vowels, half open on consonants, closed on spaces. Pluma talks only at a few moments, and never during a call.

6 · Sizes

At small sizes, the hole does the work

Below 24 pixels, the eyes are too small. The simple form shows only the nib, the slit, and the hole, and it draws the hole and the slit larger. The hole shape still tells the state: round for rest, open for a call, heart for a win, keyhole for privacy.

Menu bar, simple form

The hole is a real hole

The mouth and the slit are cut out of the shape, not painted on. They show the background in the light and the dark appearance, and in a one-color menu bar icon.

7 · Rules for the mouth

How the mouth behaves

A hole, not lipsNo teeth, no tongue, no outline. The mouth is always a hole in the metal.
Round is homeThe round hole is the rest face. Every other shape returns to it.
Special shapes stay rareHeart, keyhole, and teardrop appear only at their moments. If they appear often, they lose their meaning.
The meter tells the truthDuring a recording, the mouth follows the real voice level. It never moves to look alive when nobody speaks.
Slow for mood, fast for soundA change of mood takes about 250 ms. Only the level meter and talking move faster.
Ink means workInk flows down the slit only while Tinta writes the transcript or the summary.
