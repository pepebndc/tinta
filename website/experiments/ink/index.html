What Tinta does

You write the notes. Tinta writes the transcript.

Tinta has no chat and no cloud AI. The transcription, the speaker separation, and the summaries run on your Mac.

01

Notes and transcript, side by side

Write your notes while the draft transcript appears next to them. To mark a moment, insert a timestamp with one click.

  • The live draft shows during the call.
  • After the call, Tinta processes the recording on your Mac and improves the text.
  • Your notes stay separate from the transcript.

02

Speaker names

Tinta separates the voices in the call. Then it gives each voice a name.

  • Google Meet: a small Chrome extension reads who speaks.
  • Zoom and Microsoft Teams: in the desktop apps, Tinta reads who speaks from the call window (beta).
  • After the call: you can confirm, correct, merge, or split speakers.

Alex Moreno Morgan Lee Sam Rivera Sam Carter

03

Summaries, written on your Mac

After a call, the Apple on-device model writes a summary from your notes and the transcript. Your notes show what is important to you, so the summary uses them.

  • An overview
  • Key points
  • Decisions
  • Action items

Summaries need macOS 26 and Apple Intelligence. A summary can contain mistakes, so check it against the transcript.

04

Next meetings

Tinta reads the calendars on your Mac and shows your meetings of the next 7 days. You do not sign in.

  • A reminder shows 1 minute before each call.
  • Click Join and take notes. Tinta opens the call, starts the recording, and opens the notes.
  • Meet opens in Chrome. Zoom and Teams open in their apps.

Tinta must be open to show reminders. Tell everyone that you record.

And also

  • 05

    Local transcription

    Parakeet v3 runs on the Apple Neural Engine. It supports English, Spanish, and 23 other European languages. Tinta detects the language.

  • 06

    Call detection

    Tinta detects calls in Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams, and offers to record them. It stops the recording 3 seconds after you leave.

  • 07

    Your library

    Search your meetings, and put them in folders and tags. Export to Markdown, JSON, SRT, and VTT.

  • 08

    Trash

    Deleted meetings stay in the trash for 7 days. In that time, you can restore them.

  • 09

    Import from Granola

    Bring your Granola export into Tinta, with the notes, the transcripts, and the speaker names.

  • 10

    MCP server

    Connect Claude or another MCP client to your meetings when you choose to. You can undo every change. Read about MCP.

Privacy

Everything stays on your Mac.

Tinta has no server and no cloud service. After the one-time model download, it makes no network requests.

Processing
Transcription, speaker separation, and summaries run on your Mac.
Network
Tinta downloads the speech models once (about 500 MB). After that, it makes no network requests: no analytics, no crash reports, and no update checks.
Storage
SQLCipher encrypts the library. AES-GCM encrypts the audio. The key is in your macOS Keychain.
Audio
By default, Tinta deletes the audio 7 days after a meeting. In Settings, you can set 0 to 30 days.
Summaries
The Apple on-device model writes the summaries. Tinta does not use Private Cloud Compute or any other server.
Browser
The Meet extension reads only participant names, who speaks, and whether your microphone is muted. It does not read captions, chat, or audio.
Zoom and Teams
Tinta detects a call from the use of the microphone. With names from Zoom and Teams (beta), it reads the names and who speaks from the call window. It does not keep other text of the window.
MCP
MCP access is local, and you turn it on. An AI client that reads your meetings sends that content to its own model provider.

Tinta does not tell other people in a call that you record. Always tell them.

How it works

Five parts on one Mac.

The helpers connect to the app over a private Unix socket. The app accepts only the signed helpers inside its own bundle.

MCP

Give your meetings to Claude, when you choose to.

Tinta includes an MCP server. Open MCP in the sidebar, and turn on “Allow MCP clients to read and change meetings”.

Claude Code
claude mcp add tinta /Applications/Tinta.app/Contents/MacOS/tinta-mcp
Claude Desktop configuration
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "tinta": { "command": "/Applications/Tinta.app/Contents/MacOS/tinta-mcp" }
  }
}
  • The tools can list, search, and read meetings, with the notes, the transcripts, and the summaries.
  • They can also change titles, tags, folders, notes, summaries, speaker names, and transcript text.
  • Tinta records every MCP change. On the MCP screen, you can undo each change.
  • Deletions through MCP go to the trash.
  • Tinta must be open.

Meeting text can contain instructions from other people. Do not let an AI client act on them.

Requirements

What you need

For Tinta

  • A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14.2 or later. We recommend 16 GB of memory.
  • About 1 GB of free disk space for the app and the speech models.

For some features

  • Google Chrome, for speaker names on Google Meet.
  • The Zoom Workplace or Microsoft Teams desktop app, for Zoom or Teams calls. Tinta does not read Zoom or Teams calls in a browser.
  • macOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence, for summaries.

Install

Build Tinta from source

Tinta has no signed download yet. You build it on your Mac.

  1. Install the Xcode Command Line Tools, Rust, Node.js 20 or later, and pnpm. To install the Xcode Command Line Tools, run:

    xcode-select --install

  2. Clone the repository:

    git clone https://github.com/pepebndc/tinta.git && cd tinta

  3. Build the app:

    ./scripts/build.sh

  4. Move target/release/bundle/macos/Tinta.app to your Applications folder.

  5. Open Tinta. macOS blocks apps without Apple notarization. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.

  6. If macOS asks whether Tinta can use its key in the Keychain, click Always Allow.

Then the first-run setup guides you: your name, the speech models, the microphone, and the Chrome extension.

Questions

Questions and answers

Is Tinta free?

Yes. Tinta is free and open source, with the Apache-2.0 license.

Does Tinta send my meetings to a server?

No. Tinta processes and keeps everything on your Mac. After the one-time model download, it makes no network requests. If you connect an MCP client, that client sends what it reads to its own model provider.

Which languages can Tinta transcribe?

English, Spanish, and 23 other European languages. Tinta detects the language. Summaries support English, Spanish, and the other Apple Intelligence languages.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

Only for summaries. Summaries need macOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence turned on. Transcripts and speaker names work on macOS 14.2 or later.

Can Tinta read Zoom or Teams calls in a browser?

No. For Zoom and Teams, Tinta works with the desktop apps. For Google Meet, it works with Chrome and the Tinta extension.

Why is there no download?

The app has no Developer ID signature or Apple notarization yet. For now, you build it from source.

Is Tinta ready for daily use?

Tinta is a pilot. Expect rough edges, and export the meetings that matter. To report a bug or suggest an improvement, open an issue on GitHub.
